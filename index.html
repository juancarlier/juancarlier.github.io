About News Research Education Experience & Leadership Awards

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland, working in the GAMMA Lab with Professor Dinesh Manocha.

My research explores computer vision and learning for autonomous robotic systems, with a focus on scene understanding, reliable perception, and navigation in dynamic environments.

I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering from Michigan State University in May 2026, with a second major in Computational and Applied Mathematics and a concentration in Machine Learning. Previously, I worked with Professor D. Morris in the Smart Sensing Lab and Professor S. Bogosyan at Istanbul Technical University's LivingLab.

My research interests include:

  • Visual perception & localizationScene reconstruction, visual place recognition, and 3D perception for autonomous systems.
  • Robot navigationSocial navigation and efficient reasoning in crowded, dynamic environments.
  • Reliable autonomyRobustness under domain shift, mixed-reality testing, and operation under real-world uncertainty.

Research Experience

GAMMA Lab · University of Maryland

Ph.D. Student · August 2026–Present

Advisor: Professor Dinesh Manocha

Investigating computer vision and learning methods for scene understanding, robot navigation, and visual environmental monitoring. Studying robust perception for unmanned ground vehicles and autonomous vehicles, alongside social navigation and efficient reasoning in dynamic environments.

Smart Sensing Lab · Michigan State University

Research Scholar · September 2024–May 2026

Advisor: Professor D. Morris

Developed a real-time visual localization system for autonomous race cars targeting sub-meter accuracy. Worked on visual place recognition under domain shift using vision transformers, foundation models, and semantic segmentation. Collaborated with PoliMOVE-MSU on efficient 3D object detection for the Indy Autonomous Challenge.

LivingLab · Istanbul Technical University

Research Fellow · May–August 2025

Advisor: Professor S. Bogosyan

Integrated 3D perception and localization algorithms for autonomous driving. Led the design of a mixed-reality testing and training platform combining vehicle dynamics, sensor footage, and Gaussian splatting, with SEMBAS failure-frontier search for edge-case mining. Developed experiments to evaluate the sim-to-real gap across virtual-asset fidelity levels.
